📚 Learn about T1D

🧬

What is Type 1 diabetes?

Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition in which the body makes little or no insulin. Insulin treatment is essential.

🩸

Glucose awareness

Glucose readings can change with food, activity, illness, stress, and insulin. Follow your own care plan.

💉

Insulin safety

Use insulin only as prescribed. Follow the product's storage instructions and ask your healthcare team if uncertain.

💙

You are not alone

T1D does not define your abilities or dreams. Support from family, school, and healthcare professionals matters.

🚨 Emergency awareness

Possible low blood glucose

Follow your personal hypoglycaemia plan. If the person is unconscious, having a seizure, or unable to swallow safely, do not give food or drink by mouth. Get emergency medical help immediately.

Possible diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)

Vomiting, abdominal pain, deep or difficult breathing, unusual drowsiness, or confusion can be warning signs. Seek urgent medical care.

Never use this website to decide or change insulin doses. Follow your clinician's instructions.

💙 A simple daily reminder

The 4–3–2–1–0 reminder

Use this as a personal encouragement routine, not a medical rule. Choose habits that fit your own care plan.

  • 4 — Take four moments to pause and check in with yourself.
  • 3 — Remember three people or things that support you.
  • 2 — Name two things you learned today.
  • 1 — Choose one small positive action.
  • 0 — Aim for zero shame: T1D is not your fault.
